About Us

Beneath every wave is a story most people never get to read. Sagar Mitra is an ocean intelligence ecosystem built to bridge autonomous deep-sea science with frontline coastal communities, researchers, and maritime operators.

We stream raw ocean vitals captured by robotic profiling floats through statistical baseline models and AI anomaly detectors. By translating complex ocean physics into accessible real-time guidance, Sagar Mitra transforms massive observational datasets into dependable tools that fishermen, navigators, and scientists can rely on every day.

ARGO Floats

Argo floats are autonomous profiling robotic probes that drift freely with global ocean currents, diving to depths of 2,000 metres into the ocean abyss.

Over continuous 10-day operational cycles, each probe descends, drifts silently along isobaric deep-sea currents, and records high-precision vertical profiles of salinity, temperature, and pressure as it ascends. The instant the probe resurfaces, its antenna beams the synthesized data packet home via satellite.
