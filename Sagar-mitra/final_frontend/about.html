About Sagar Mitra

Sagar Mitra is our vision for making the ocean easier to understand, explore and interact with. Built around the ORCA SIH problem statement, our platform brings together marine, satellite Earth Observation, weather, geospatial and ecological information through an intelligent conversational interface.

Instead of forcing users to search through disconnected datasets, Sagar Mitra understands natural-language questions, identifies what information is needed, and uses collaborative AI agents to divide complex tasks among specialized agents. These agents retrieve relevant data, reason across locations and time, combine their findings and return an explainable result through conversations, maps and visualizations.

Our goal is to turn complex ocean data into meaningful ocean intelligence, helping students learn, researchers investigate, fishermen navigate, coastal communities stay informed, authorities monitor marine environments, and explorers discover more about the ocean and its life.
