About Us

Beneath every wave is a story most people never get to read. Sagar Mitra listens to it through a fleet of autonomous ARGO floats and log various parameters at every metre and send the finished profile home the moment they resurface. We run that raw signal through a statistical baseline model tuned to catch different anomalies, turning a dataset into something a fisherman, student or researcher can use.

ARGO Floats

Argo floats are autonomous profiling probes that capture vertical ocean measurements using sensors to record pressure, temperature, and salinity. Over a series of 10 continuous days, they go to a depth of 2000m in the ocean and while coming back, capture th readings required for our dataset.
